Confidence Interval Capture Simulator

A population has a true mean of μ = 50 (standard deviation σ = 10) — in real life we would never get to see it. Each sample produces a confidence interval: sample mean ± margin of error. Green intervals captured the true mean; red ones missed. A 95% confidence level is a promise about the method: over many samples, about 95% of the intervals it builds will capture μ.
